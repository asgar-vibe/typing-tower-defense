2. **Tactical Panic Words (Emergency Commands):**
   Players can activate emergency interventions by typing uppercase reserved trigger words when not locked onto a target, or via an emergency prefix (e.g., typing `!` or pressing `Space` to enter Command Mode):

     - Spawns a full-screen white/amber flash and screen shake.
   - **`FREEZE` (Cost: 150g or 50 Energy):**
     - Completely halts all enemy movement across all lanes for 5.0 seconds.
     - Adds a frosted ice visual overlay across the arena.
   - **`REPAIR` (Cost: 100g):**
     - Instantly restores +25 HP to the Fortress Core (capped at max HP).
