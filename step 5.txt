### Detailed Specifications:

1. **Procedural Web Audio Engine (`src/utils/SoundEngine.ts`):**
   Use standard browser `AudioContext` (no external mp3/wav files required) to synthesize sounds on the fly:
   - **`playKeyClick()`:** A sharp, tactile mechanical switch click (short sine/triangle blip with slight pitch variation: ~800Hz-1200Hz, duration: 0.03s).
   - **`playLaserShot()`:** Quick frequency sweep downwards (1500Hz down to 300Hz, sawtooth wave, duration: 0.08s).
   - **`playExplosion()`:** Filtered white noise burst with low-pass roll-off to simulate an explosive blast (duration: 0.3s).
   - **`playTypoError()`:** Low discordant sawtooth buzz (150Hz, duration: 0.1s).
   - **`playNukeAlarm()`:** Descending alarm tone with heavy sub-bass boom.
   - **`playFreezeSound()`:** High-frequency crystalline shimmer (sine wave modulated with noise).
   - *Audio unlock:* Initialize AudioContext gracefully on the user's first click or keystroke.

2. **Canvas / SVG Particle Emitter (`ParticleOverlay.tsx`):**
   - Render a lightweight overlay canvas on top of `DefenseArena`:
     - **On Correct Letter Typed:** Emit 3-5 tiny sparks flying back from the hit point.
     - **On Enemy Defeat:** Explode 25-40 neon debris particles radiating outward with random velocities, gravity decay, and fading opacity.
     - **On Gold Earned:** Floating bounce text `+25G` floating upward and fading out.
     - **On Freeze Cast:** Shimmering frost particles drifting slowly across the entire screen.

3. **Screen Shake & Dynamic Post-Processing:**

     - **Light Trauma (Typo / Laser hit):** 2px shake for 100ms.
     - **Heavy Trauma (Enemy breaches base):** 8-12px violent shake for 400ms accompanied by a pulsing red vignette border on the viewport.
     - **Nuke Impact:** Full screen white-out flash fading out over 0.5s with a rolling rumble.

4. **Game Over & Wave Victory Modals:**
